GTA 6 Radio Stations: All 23 Stations, Hosts and the Soundtrack

Grand Theft Auto VI has 22 radio stations on the dial from the start, 20 playing music and two playing talk, plus a hidden 23rd station, The Vault Radio, that you unlock inside a Vice City nightclub. Rockstar calls it “the biggest and widest-ranging selection of in-game radio yet”, and most stations are hosted by real artists playing on-air characters, from Bad Bunny on Cocoteo FM to Keith Richards on the blues station. This guide lists every GTA 6 radio station with its music and hosts, shows which stations return from older games, and explains the two other music releases: GTA VI: The Album and the original score by Justice.

The short version: Rockstar officially introduced seven stations before launch. The full list of 23 and the real names of the hosts come from the game’s credits, as reported by launch coverage. For the first time in the series you can also listen on foot through headphones or earbuds, and play on-demand podcasts. The 34-track album is out now, and the Justice score follows on December 11. Everything here is as of November 21, 2026.

GTA 6 Radio at a Glance

The music in Grand Theft Auto VI comes in three separate parts, and a lot of coverage mixes them up. The radio is what you hear in the game while driving or walking. The Album is a 34-song record of new tracks made for the game. The score is the background music Justice wrote for missions and the open world.

Before launch, Rockstar named a few genres the radio covers, “from Classic Rock to Talk Radio”. With the game out, the dial also covers blues, jazz, classical, reggae, salsa and more.

Every GTA 6 Radio Station and Host

The table lists all 23 stations, grouped the same way. Hosts are shown with their on-air name in brackets where the character has a different name. Rockstar’s own posts only use the on-air names, such as El Martillo and La Gata, while the real names come from the game’s credits.

Some stations also carry shows with their own hosts. Honey FM runs Liquid City with Craig Robinson as Liquid Wilson, and VCRR has Early AF with Charlamagne tha God and Ivy Rivera.

How Many Radio Stations Are in GTA 6?

You will see two numbers online, 22 and 23, and both are right. GameSpot counts 22, the stations you can tune to as soon as you start, and calls it “the most that Rockstar has ever included in a Grand Theft Auto title”. Guides that count 23 also include The Vault Radio, which stays hidden until you unlock it. Rockstar itself hasn’t published a total, only that the selection is its biggest yet.

The Seven Stations Rockstar Previewed

On October 8, 2026, Rockstar’s Newswire introduced six stations, each with an audio preview on the official Music page. CircoLoco Records Radio was added to the same page later, which makes seven. These are the only stations Rockstar has described in its own words so far.

Back Country Radio also has a third voice. Rockstar calls Mud Dog Mike the station’s “roving reporter, swamp meteorologist” and Delta Dawne’s “not-so-secret lover”, and the credits list him as Jeremy Dufrene, Lana Del Rey’s husband.

Stations Returning From Older GTA Games

GTA 6 is set in Vice City again, and a few familiar station names come back with it. The stations are new in every other way: each one has new hosts and a new playlist.

  • Flash FM, V-Rock and Emotion 98.3 were all stations in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City (2002). Flash FM was the pop station then too, and V-Rock and Emotion 98.3 keep their rock and soft-rock sounds.
  • WKTT, short for “We Know The Truth”, is the talk station from Grand Theft Auto IV.
  • Worldwide FM was a station in Grand Theft Auto V, and Gilles Peterson hosts it again.

Long-time players will notice which stations didn’t make it. There is no Non-Stop-Pop FM or Los Santos Rock Radio from GTA V on the dial.

How to Unlock The Vault Radio

The Vault Radio is the one station not on the dial when you start. It plays electronic music hosted by DJ Pretzel, and launch guides report the same way to get it:

  1. Go to The Vault, a nightclub in Vice City, and walk inside.
  2. Find the poster on the wall inside the club.
  3. Scan it with the camera on your phone. The station then shows up on the radio in cars and through headphones.

Rockstar hasn’t explained the unlock itself, so these steps come from players and guides after launch.

Talk Radio, Podcasts and Listening on Foot

Talk radio has always been where the GTA series puts its satire, and GTA 6 has two talk stations with several shows each.

WKTT and The Wipe

  • WKTT carries The Richard Bastion Show with Jason Sudeikis as Richard Bastion, and Hit the Bricks with Stavros Halkias as Dominic “The Brick” Brizzolato.
  • The Wipe carries Ask Dr. Nita with Anitta, El Otro Lado with John Leguizamo as Enrique “El Enigma” Romero, and She Got Assets with Janelle James as Tish.

Podcasts and Headphones

Two things are new to the series, both confirmed by Rockstar. First, GTA 6 has a set of on-demand podcasts that Jason and Lucia can play, separate from the radio stations. Second, you no longer need a car. Both the radio and the podcasts play “in vehicles and on foot through newly introduced headphones and earbuds connected to their smartphones”. So you can keep a station going while you walk around Vice City.

GTA VI: The Album Tracklist

GTA VI: The Album is the official soundtrack album, made with Atlantic Records. Its 34 tracks are new songs written for the game, not a collection of the licensed songs on the radio. Rockstar announced it for November 19, the game’s launch day, and its Newswire post on November 20 confirmed it is “available everywhere now” on the major streaming services. The tracklist below is in album order, as listed on Rockstar’s Music page.

Which Album Songs Play on the Radio

Several album tracks also turn up on the in-game stations, according to the station tracklists reported at launch. Travis Scott’s RHYNO and Cardi B’s Don’t Chart play on VCRR, Morgan Wallen’s Last Thing You Need is on Back Country Radio, Suzuki and Macacoa 2000 are on Cocoteo FM, and Overtown Lights is on Leonida Jazz Radio. So a few of the songs you hear while driving are on the album too, but most of the radio is licensed music that isn’t.

Vinyl and CD Editions

The Album is also out on limited-edition vinyl and CD. Rockstar said three editions are only sold at gtavi-thealbum.com: a liquid-filled vinyl, a splatter vinyl and a CD jewel case. Other vinyl colourways with their own artwork are sold at stores including Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Target, Barnes & Noble, Urban Outfitters and Blood Records.

GTA VI: The Festival in Miami

To celebrate the launch, Rockstar and PlayStation hold Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival on November 21, 2026 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, with two stages of artists from the game and the album. The lineup Rockstar announced includes Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black and Sofaygo under Cactus Jack, plus Cardi B, CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, a Justice DJ set, Metro Boomin and Tainy. Tickets were sold through a pre-sale lottery that closed on November 10.

The Original Score by Justice

The score is composed by Justice, the Grammy-winning French duo of Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay. Rockstar calls it an adaptive score, “dynamically tuned to the different ways players engage with the world”, so the music in a scene changes with how you play it. It is separate from the radio: the score plays as background music in the game, not on a station.

The score album comes out on December 11, 2026 on streaming services, CD and vinyl, including a Limited Triple Crystal Clear vinyl and a double CD. It has 30 tracks plus some digital bonus tracks. Three singles are out already:

  • Flower in a Hurricane (feat. Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard), the song from the launch trailer
  • Pusher (feat. Gesaffelstein), released on October 29
  • Starting from Zero (feat. Michael McDonald), which also opens The Album

Frequently Asked Questions

How many radio stations are in GTA 6?

22 are available from the start, 20 music and two talk. A 23rd, The Vault Radio, is hidden until you scan a poster inside The Vault nightclub in Vice City.

Who are the celebrity DJs in GTA 6?

They include Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen, Burna Boy, Robyn, Keith Richards, Buddy Guy, Gloria Estefan and “Weird Al” Yankovic. Most host under an on-air name, like Bad Bunny as El Martillo.

Can you listen to the radio while walking in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar added headphones and earbuds that connect to Jason and Lucia’s phones, so the radio and the on-demand podcasts play on foot as well as in vehicles.

Are Flash FM and V-Rock back?

Yes. Flash FM, V-Rock and Emotion 98.3 return from 2002’s GTA Vice City with new hosts and playlists. WKTT returns from GTA IV and Worldwide FM from GTA V.

Where can I stream the GTA 6 soundtrack?

GTA VI: The Album, with 34 new tracks, is on all major music streaming services. It is also sold on vinyl and CD at gtavi-thealbum.com and select retailers.

When does the Justice score for GTA 6 come out?

December 11, 2026. The Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score has 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks, on streaming services, CD and vinyl.
