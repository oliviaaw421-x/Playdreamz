Dave the Diver DLC Guide: Every Content Pack, Price & How to Start (2026)

Dave the Diver has grown a lot since launch. There are now six content packs, from free crossovers with DREDGE and Godzilla to the paid In the Jungle expansion, plus one more on the way. This Dave the Diver DLC guide covers every pack: what it adds, what it costs, how to start it, and whether it’s worth your time.

Heads up: two packs leave the stores on December 31, 2026. The free Godzilla Content Pack and the paid Ichiban’s Holiday Content Pack can only be claimed or bought until then. If you get them before the deadline, you keep them forever.

Every Dave the Diver DLC at a Glance

Here is everything available as of October 2026. Prices are the US Steam prices. Console prices are usually the same or close.

All of these are optional. None of them is needed to finish the main story, and none of them locks you out of anything in the base game.

Which DLC Should You Get?

  • Grab every free pack now. DREDGE, Godzilla and Guilty Gear Strive cost nothing. Godzilla is the urgent one, because the free claim ends on December 31, 2026.
  • Want a lot more game? Buy In the Jungle. It is the only full expansion, with over 10 hours of new story, a new map and a second restaurant.
  • Love Like a Dragon, or just want a fun side story? Buy Ichiban’s Holiday before it leaves the stores at the end of 2026.
  • Still on your first playthrough? Don’t worry about the DLC yet. Most of it unlocks mid-game or later, so install the packs and they will trigger on their own as you progress. New to the game? Start with our Dave the Diver beginner’s guide.

In the Jungle (Paid)

In the Jungle is the biggest Dave the Diver DLC by far. It takes place after the main story: Dave and the crew leave the Blue Hole and travel to Utara, a jungle village where something strange has been reported. Instead of the ocean, you dive into a freshwater lake with a whole new ecosystem.

What’s New

  • A freshwater lake. New fish, new ingredients and new dangers, including crocodiles and piranhas. Deep down, there are ruins from an ancient underwater civilization.
  • Bancho Grill. A second restaurant with a new menu. Unlike Bancho Sushi, you can walk freely around the restaurant and serve customers in several areas.
  • Utara Village. Help the villagers and build friendships with them. Some are wary of outsiders at first, but gifts and good meals win them over, and friends may show up at Bancho Grill as guests.
  • The Jungle Gun. Your main weapon in the DLC. It can switch between most of the gun types from the main game.
  • More to explore. Ancient temples, eerie forests and a set of new mini-games across the jungle.

How to Start In the Jungle

First, buy and install the content pack and update the game. You’ll know it worked when the title screen switches to a jungle theme. Then pick one of two ways in:

  1. Continue from a finished save (recommended). Load a save that has reached the Epilogue. Get on the boat at the Blue Hole and wait for Dr. Bacon to show up, which gives you the first mission, “To a New Place.” Then go to Bancho Sushi, watch the short scene, and call Dr. Bacon to set off. If he doesn’t appear, do a dive or skip time and check again.
  2. Skip to Jungle. On the title screen, choose New Game → Skip to Jungle. This works even if you never finished the main game. Be careful: it overwrites your autosave, so back up any save you want to keep, and you can’t earn main-game achievements on that file.

Tip: the jungle enemies hit harder than the Blue Hole’s. If you’re coming from a finished save, upgrade your air tank and carry weight before calling Dr. Bacon.

Is In the Jungle Worth It?

Yes, if you loved the restaurant and village side of Dave the Diver. For $9.99 you get a new map, a new restaurant and more than 10 hours of story. A common complaint in reviews is that there’s less diving than in the main game, with more time spent in the village and restaurant. If diving was your favourite part, keep that in mind.

Ichiban’s Holiday (Paid, Time-Limited)

This crossover with SEGA’s Like a Dragon series brings Ichiban Kasuga and the Survive Bar bartender to the Blue Hole for a holiday. It doesn’t stay relaxing for long: a dolphin-poaching syndicate is causing trouble, and Ichiban and Dave dive in to stop it.

  • A side story starring Ichiban, written in the same goofy, big-hearted style as the Like a Dragon games.
  • A beat-’em-up mini-game where Ichiban fights alongside Cobra.
  • New mini-games that Like a Dragon fans will recognise, including karaoke.
  • New staff for Bancho Sushi.

How to start: buy and install the pack, then finish Chapter 5 (Melting Glacier). Once Chapter 6 begins, Ichiban and the bartender visit Bancho Sushi during evening service. The story kicks off after they leave the restaurant.

Worth it? If you’re a Like a Dragon fan, absolutely. If not, it’s still a fun, silly side story for the price of a sandwich. Just decide before December 31, 2026, when it leaves the stores.

Godzilla Content Pack (Free, Time-Limited)

The Godzilla pack first came out free in May 2024 and was pulled from stores that November. It returned on June 27, 2025, and the store listings now say it can be claimed for free until December 31, 2026. Once you claim it, it stays in your library for good, even after the offer ends.

  • Three Godzilla missions that play out after Chapter 5.
  • New dishes for Bancho Sushi.
  • Collectible figures hidden around the ocean, so keep your eyes open on your dives.

How to start: claim and install the pack, then fully complete Chapter 5, which includes opening the Glacial Passage near the Sea People’s Village. On the next in-game day, a cutscene shows an earthquake and a strange noise from the depths. That’s Godzilla.

Note for Steam players: Steam only lets you claim the pack if you already own Dave the Diver. On consoles you can claim it without the base game, which is handy if you plan to buy the game later.

DREDGE Content Pack (Free)

The first Dave the Diver crossover, revealed at The Game Awards 2023 and released on December 15, 2023. It brings the eerie fishing horror of DREDGE to the Blue Hole.

  • A new Thick Fog weather event that opens up a night-time map.
  • A boat-driving section where you pick your dive spots.
  • Aberrations: twisted, mutated fish from the DREDGE world.
  • The Drain Gun, a weapon crafted from aberrant material.
  • New recipes, plus a mysterious new character from DREDGE.

How to start: download the free pack first (on Steam it’s listed separately under the game’s DLC). Then play until you unlock the Chicken Farm. A few in-game days later, foggy nights start to appear. Check the weather app on Dave’s phone for a Thick Fog forecast, then skip to that night to begin.

Guilty Gear Strive Content Pack (Free)

This pack used to be exclusive to the Nintendo Switch Anniversary Edition. In September 2026, MINTROCKET released it for free on PC, PlayStation and Xbox. It’s a small bundle of extras rather than a story pack:

  • A rhythm game set to the Guilty Gear Strive track “The Disaster of Passion.”
  • Cosplay customers dressed as Guilty Gear fighters at Bancho Sushi.
  • The May Speaker, a rock-themed decoration for the restaurant.
  • May’s Anchor, a special weapon for your dives that hits with a dolphin-charged strike.

Switch and Switch 2 players without the Anniversary Edition will get the same content later in a free update. MINTROCKET hasn’t said yet whether it is coming to the iOS and Android versions.

Subnautica 2 Content Pack (Coming 2027)

Announced on October 8, 2026, this crossover with Unknown Worlds’ Subnautica 2 will be the final DLC for Dave the Diver. It’s free for everyone who owns the game and is planned for early 2027.

A visitor from the other side arrives at the Blue Hole, and Dave heads through a portal to Proteus, the ocean moon from Subnautica 2. Expect new creatures and resources, new characters, and some very unwelcome run-ins with Subnautica’s famous Leviathans. MINTROCKET describes it as a full story event, like the Ichiban crossover. You can wishlist it now on Steam, the Epic Games Store and Xbox.

What About the Digital Extra?

On Steam you may also see the Dave the Diver Digital Extra ($8.99). It isn’t gameplay content. It includes a unique skin for Cobra’s boat, a digital art book (PDF) with concept art and pixel art, and the full soundtrack with 92 tracks. It’s a nice way to support the developers, but you won’t miss anything in the game without it.

Best Order to Play the DLC

The packs unlock at different points, so they fit into one playthrough like this:

  1. Install everything at the start. The free packs and anything you bought will trigger on their own when you reach them.
  2. DREDGE appears first, on foggy nights after the Chicken Farm opens.
  3. Godzilla follows right after you finish Chapter 5.
  4. Ichiban’s Holiday starts during Chapter 6.
  5. In the Jungle comes last, once the main story is done.
  6. Subnautica 2 arrives in early 2027.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dave the Diver DLC

Is Dave the Diver DLC free?

Most of it is. The DREDGE, Godzilla and Guilty Gear Strive packs are free, and the upcoming Subnautica 2 pack will be free too. Only In the Jungle ($9.99) and Ichiban’s Holiday ($6.99) cost money.

Can I still get the Godzilla DLC?

Yes, until December 31, 2026. The Godzilla Content Pack is free to claim until then, and once you claim it you keep it permanently. On Steam you need to own Dave the Diver first.

How do I start the In the Jungle DLC?

Load a save that has reached the Epilogue, wait for Dr. Bacon to appear at the Blue Hole, then visit Bancho Sushi and call him to set off. If you haven’t finished the main game, choose New Game and then Skip to Jungle on the title screen.

Do I need to finish the main game before playing the DLC?

Only for In the Jungle, and even then you can use the Skip to Jungle option. DREDGE unlocks after the Chicken Farm, Godzilla after Chapter 5, and Ichiban’s Holiday during Chapter 6. MINTROCKET hasn’t announced a story requirement for the Guilty Gear Strive extras.

What is the last DLC for Dave the Diver?

The Subnautica 2 Content Pack. MINTROCKET announced on October 8, 2026 that it will be the game’s final DLC. It’s free and planned for early 2027.
