Cyberpunk TCG Rarity Symbols: Every Rarity in Welcome to Night City Explained

Cyberpunk TCG rarity symbols are small icons, not letter codes, so they are easy to miss on a new card. Each card in Welcome to Night City, the first Cyberpunk TCG set, has one of seven symbols at the bottom centre, from a single chevron on Commons to the spiked crest of an Iconic Rare. This guide explains every symbol, where to find it, how many cards each rarity has, WeirdCo’s official pull rates, what each rarity sells for and how the Beta (Kickstarter) and Retail editions differ.

The short version: the seven rarities, lowest to highest, are Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic Rare, Secret Rare, Iconic Rare and Nova Rare. Every 12-card pack has 7 Commons, 3 Uncommons and 2 Rare-or-better cards. Epic Rares turn up in about 1 in 4 packs, Iconic Rares in about 1 in 18 and Secret Rares in about 1 in 24. Nova Rares are promos and never come in packs. Symbols come from WeirdCo’s Comprehensive Rules, counts and odds from WeirdCo’s Kickstarter updates, and prices from TCGplayer as of October 2026.

Every Cyberpunk TCG Rarity Symbol, Explained

WeirdCo’s Comprehensive Rules list the rarities in a chart (Figure 2, section 3.16) and define rarity as “how common the card is in its printed set”. In Cyberpunk TCG, rarity has no effect on how a card plays. An Epic Rare and its Iconic Rare version are the same card in a game.

Common and Uncommon Symbols

Cyberpunk TCG Commons carry a single chevron pointing down, and Uncommons a single chevron pointing up. Together they fill 10 of the 12 cards in every pack, and they make up 92 of the 140 cards in Welcome to Night City. They rarely sell for more than a few cents, but players need plenty of them to build decks.

Rare Symbol

The Rare symbol puts the two chevrons together, one pointing up and one pointing down, like an open diamond. Rares fill most of the two Rare-or-better slots in a pack, and WeirdCo says they appear “more than once per pack”. Many Legends start at Rare, including V: Streetkid, Goro Takemura: Vengeful Bodyguard and Alt Cunningham: Soulkiller Architect. The official card database lists V: Streetkid twice, as 005a and 005b, which is why some card lists count 33 Rares instead of 32.

What Is an Epic Rare?

An Epic Rare adds a small solid diamond inside the Rare chevrons. There are 12 Epic Rares in the first Cyberpunk TCG set, and you’ll find one in about one pack in four. They include some of the biggest names from Cyberpunk 2077, such as Adam Smasher: Ender of Legends, Johnny Silverhand: Never Stop Fighting and Judy Álvarez: Braindance Maestro.

What Is a Secret Rare?

A Secret Rare takes the Epic symbol and places it between two large brackets, so it looks wider than the others. Only four cards in the set are Secret Rares, and each one turns up in about one pack in 24, which makes them the hardest base rarity to pull from a pack.

What Is an Iconic Rare in Cyberpunk TCG?

An Iconic Rare is a foiled, alternate-art version of a card that already exists at a lower rarity. Its symbol is the Epic diamond with spikes at the top and bottom, like a crest. Welcome to Night City has 31 Iconic Rares in three groups, according to WeirdCo:

  • Legend Iconic Rares: alternate art for 18 of the 20 Legends in the set. The other two Legends are Secret Rares.
  • Core Iconic Rares: alternate art for 9 Units, Gear and Programs.
  • Secret Iconic Rares: alternate art for the four Secret Rares. These are the top chase cards in the set.

What Is a Nova Rare?

A Nova Rare has a solid square inside a striped frame. It is the only rarity you can’t pull from a booster pack. Nova Rares come from box toppers, prerelease kits, tournaments and other promotions, which are covered in the Nova Rare section below. Being last in WeirdCo’s chart doesn’t make a Nova Rare the most valuable card. It only means the card is a promo.

Is Iconic Rare Rarer Than Secret Rare?

Not per pack. WeirdCo’s chart lists Iconic above Secret, but its own odds put a Secret Rare at about 1 in 24 packs and an Iconic Rare at about 1 in 18. Any one Iconic Rare is still very hard to find, though, because the Iconic slot is shared by 31 cards while the Secret slot is shared by only four. The Secret Iconic Rares combine both: they are Iconic versions of the four Secret Rares.

Where Is the Rarity Symbol on a Cyberpunk TCG Card?

The rarity symbol sits at the bottom centre of the card, inside a small bracket, just left of the “© 2026 CD PROJEKT S.A.” line. It takes the colour of the card’s frame, so a red card has a red symbol and a blue card a blue one. Three other details along the edges help you identify a Cyberpunk TCG card:

  • The collector number in the yellow tab at the bottom left, like 001. Iconic Rares are numbered after the main set, from 141 to 171.
  • The product code printed up the left edge, like MS01 - WNC [A].
  • The illustrator credit up the right edge. Iconic Rares usually have a different artist from the base card.

What Does MS01 - WNC [A] Mean?

The Comprehensive Rules (section 3.14) split the code on the left edge into three parts: the product type (MS01), the product code (WNC, short for Welcome to Night City) and the block code ([A]). Promo cards use a different product type. The Nova Rare box toppers, for example, read PRM - WNC [A].

How to Tell Beta and Retail Cards Apart

To tell Beta and Retail Cyberpunk TCG cards apart, look at the collector number. Beta (Kickstarter) printings put a β in front of it, so the Beta copy of card 001 reads β001. Retail cards have the number alone. Iconic Rares are also easy to tell apart, because each edition has its own artwork, as explained in Beta vs Retail below.

The 31 Iconic Rares

Numbers 141 to 158 are the Legend Iconic Rares: Adam Smasher, Dexter DeShawn, Royce, V, Dum Dum, Kerry Eurodyne, Muamar Reyes, River Ward, Rogue Amendiares, Goro Takemura, Hanako Arasaka, Jackie Welles, Padre, Panam Palmer, Alt Cunningham, Evelyn Parker, Judy Álvarez and Wakako Okada. Numbers 159 to 167 are the Core Iconic Rares, alt-arts of Johnny Silverhand: Never Stop Fighting, Adam Smasher: Metal Over Meat, Alt Cunningham: Mother of Daemons, Hanako Arasaka: In a Gilded Cage, The Relic, Sandayu Oda, Overwatch, Rogue Amendiares: Queen of the Afterlife and Towerfall. Numbers 168 to 171 are the Secret Iconic Rares of Johnny Silverhand, Sasha Yakovleva, Panam Palmer and Judy Álvarez.

WeirdCo has confirmed 31 Iconic Rares for Retail as well as Beta. As of late October 2026, the official card database lists 28 of the 31 Retail ones, with three Secret Iconic Rares still to be added.

Cyberpunk TCG Pull Rates

Unlike Bandai with the One Piece Card Game, WeirdCo has published official Cyberpunk TCG pull rates. Its Kickstarter update in April 2026 gave the pack contents and odds, and said that Beta and Retail packs share “the same chances of pulling a particular rarity”. Each Cyberpunk TCG booster pack contains 12 cards:

  • 7 Common cards
  • 3 Uncommon cards
  • 2 Rare+ cards, each a Rare, Epic Rare, Secret Rare or Iconic Rare

What to Expect From a Booster Box

The box figures above are averages worked out from WeirdCo’s odds, not promises. WeirdCo says “around” for every rate, so one box can come up short while another has extras. On average, a 24-pack Cyberpunk TCG Retail box gives you about six Epic Rares, one Secret Rare and one or two Iconic Rares. A 36-pack Beta box gives you about half as many again.

WeirdCo hasn’t given separate odds for the four Secret Iconic Rares, or for any single card. With 31 Iconic Rares sharing roughly one slot in 18 packs, any one Iconic is far harder to find than the overall rate suggests. If you want a particular card, buying the single is almost always cheaper than opening packs for it.

What Each Cyberpunk TCG Rarity Is Worth

Retail Cyberpunk TCG singles don’t have a market yet, because the Retail edition only reaches stores on November 6, 2026. Beta cards have been trading since they shipped to Kickstarter backers, so they give the best early picture. These are TCGplayer market prices for Welcome to Night City Beta cards as of October 2026, showing the middle half of each rarity:

A sealed Beta booster box sold for about $319 on TCGplayer in October 2026, well above the $180 retail price WeirdCo announced for Beta displays, as reported by ICv2. Retail boxes were listed on TCGplayer at about $182. Prices for a brand-new game like Cyberpunk TCG move quickly in its first months, so check recent sold listings before you buy or sell.

Same Card, Different Rarity

The rarity symbol matters more than the card name. The Beta Epic Rare Judy Álvarez: Braindance Maestro sold for under $1 in October 2026, while her Iconic Rare version was around $350. Sasha Yakovleva: Won’t Let You Down shows an even bigger gap, at about $12 as a Secret Rare and over $1,000 as a Secret Iconic Rare. When you search for a price, include the collector number or the rarity, like “Sasha Yakovleva Iconic”, and compare sold listings, not asking prices.

The Most Valuable Welcome to Night City Cards

As of October 2026, the most valuable Cyberpunk TCG cards on TCGplayer are the four Beta Secret Iconic Rares, each at about $750 to $1,110: Johnny Silverhand: Rocking Renegade (β168), Sasha Yakovleva: Won’t Let You Down (β169), Judy Álvarez: Nothing to Doubt (β171) and Panam Palmer: Strength Through Family (β170). After them come the Legend Iconic Rares of Judy Álvarez (about $354), Alt Cunningham (about $250) and Hanako Arasaka (about $241). Popular characters such as Johnny Silverhand, Judy Álvarez and Panam Palmer lead the list, much as Charizard and Pikachu do in Pokémon.

Beta vs Retail: What’s Different?

Welcome to Night City comes in two editions. The Beta edition went to Kickstarter backers, and the Retail edition launches in stores, with prereleases from October 30 and the full release on November 6, 2026, according to ICv2. WeirdCo says the two Cyberpunk TCG editions play the same:

  • Same cards: the 140 mechanically unique cards are identical in both editions.
  • Different Iconic Rares: each edition has 31 Iconic Rares with its own artwork, so there are 62 in all. WeirdCo said it gave Retail its own Iconic Rares to support local game stores.
  • Different numbering: Beta collector numbers start with β.
  • Different box sizes: Beta displays hold 36 packs and Retail displays 24. Pack contents and odds are the same.

The official card database also lists a French printing of the Retail set, so the game isn’t English-only.

Nova Rare Cards and Promos

Nova Rares are the Cyberpunk TCG promo rarity. The official card database files them under these products:

  • Box toppers: six for Beta and six for Retail, including Yorinobu Arasaka: Embracing Destruction and V: Corporate Exile. Box toppers are bonus cards that come with a booster display rather than inside a pack.
  • Prerelease kits: one Nova Rare promo for each edition. ICv2 reports that Retail prerelease kits contain 6 packs, 3 Legends, dice and a limited promo.
  • Organized play: dozens of Nova Rare printings for Edgerunner Open, Night City Brawl and Night City Showdown events.
  • Set 1 promos: four more Nova Rare printings from other promotions.

Several of the box-topper characters, such as Saburo Arasaka and Viktor Vektor’s “Sit Down and Relax” card, can’t be pulled from Welcome to Night City packs at all. Because supply depends on how many events and boxes there are, Nova Rare prices vary widely. In October 2026, the Beta prerelease promo Mantis Blades sold for about $8 on TCGplayer.

Protecting Your Best Pulls

Sleeve every Epic, Secret and Iconic Rare as soon as it leaves the pack, and put anything worth more than a few dollars in a toploader. Iconic Rares are foiled, so they show scratches and bends easily. The storage rules are the same for every card game, so our guide on how to store Pokémon cards works for Cyberpunk TCG too. For the expensive Iconic Rares, professional grading can help when you sell. One Kickstarter reward tier even included a pre-graded CGC card, and our grading guide explains how grading costs and companies compare.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do the Cyberpunk TCG rarity symbols mean?

A chevron pointing down is Common and a chevron pointing up is Uncommon. Two chevrons are Rare, and two chevrons around a small diamond are Epic Rare. The Epic symbol between two side brackets is Secret Rare, the Epic symbol with spikes at the top and bottom is Iconic Rare, and a striped square is Nova Rare.

Where is the rarity on a Cyberpunk TCG card?

At the bottom centre of the card, inside a small bracket just left of the copyright line. The collector number is in the yellow tab at the bottom left.

What is the rarest card in Welcome to Night City?

The four Secret Iconic Rares, the Iconic versions of the Secret Rares. As of October 2026, the Beta versions sell for about $750 to $1,110 each on TCGplayer.

How many cards are in a Cyberpunk TCG booster pack?

Twelve: 7 Commons, 3 Uncommons and 2 Rare-or-better cards. Retail booster displays hold 24 packs and Beta displays 36.

Are Beta and Retail Cyberpunk TCG cards different?

They play the same, but each edition has its own artwork for the 31 Iconic Rares, and Beta collector numbers start with β. Pack contents and pull rates are the same.

Can you pull Nova Rares from packs?

No. Nova Rares are promos from box toppers, prerelease kits, tournaments and other promotions. They never come in booster packs.
