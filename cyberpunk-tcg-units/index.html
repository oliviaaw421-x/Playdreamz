Cyberpunk TCG Units: All 69 Units and How Fights Work

Cyberpunk TCG Units are the crew you put on the field: gangers, mercs, netrunners, cops and cars that attack to steal your Rival’s Gigs and fight their Units. They are the biggest card type in the game. This guide lists all 69 Cyberpunk TCG Units in Welcome to Night City with pictures, plus the four that are only in the starter decks. It explains how Units are played, how fights and steals work under WeirdCo’s latest Comprehensive Rules, what Blocker and Adrenaline do, and which Units the official decklists play most.

The short version: Welcome to Night City has 69 Units: 17 Red, 18 Yellow, 17 Green and 17 Blue, from cost 2 to cost 9. A Unit can’t attack the turn it’s played unless it has Adrenaline. In a fight, the Unit with higher power wins and a tie means both lose. A Unit with power 1 to 9 steals 1 Gig and power 10 to 19 steals 2. Everything here is as of November 21, 2026.

All 69 Cyberpunk TCG Units at a Glance

Units make up almost half of Welcome to Night City, the first set: 69 of the 140 cards, ahead of 34 Programs, 20 Legends and 17 Gear. The colors are close to even, with Yellow one card ahead. Most Units cost 3 to 5, and only eight cost 7 or more. Power runs from 0 to 15.

Every Unit has one or more tags that show who it is in Night City. GANGER is the most common, on 20 Units, followed by MERC, NETRUNNER and CORPO. Tags matter because some cards check for them, such as Johnny Silverhand, who wins every fight against a CORPO Unit.

What Is a Unit in Cyberpunk TCG?

Units are the cards that stay on your field and do the fighting. Section 4 of the rules covers them in three lines:

  • Playing a Unit (4.6): “reveal the card to your Rival, pay its cost, and place it ready in the field area.”
  • Lag (4.7): Units enter the field with Lag. A lagging Unit can’t attack and can’t use its Spend effects. All Units lose Lag at the end of each turn (11.3).
  • Attacking (4.8): you spend a Unit to declare an attack.

In practice, that means a Unit you play this turn usually attacks next turn. Your Units also defend you: a ready Unit with Blocker can step in front of an attack, and a spent Unit is a target your Rival can attack.

Units aren’t the only cards that attack. A Legend with Go Solo can be played as a ready Unit that attacks the same turn. Our Cyberpunk TCG Legends guide covers how that works.

How Units Fight and Steal

To attack, you spend a ready, non-lagging Unit and pick a valid target (9.3). There are only two kinds of target:

  • A spent rival Unit. Your Unit fights it. You can’t attack a ready Unit unless a card says so.
  • A rival Gig area with at least one Gig. Your Unit steals from it.

How Fights Work

WeirdCo rewrote the fight rules for the Retail release, in its October 29, 2026 update (section 11.4). The Unit with higher power wins and the other loses. “If both Units in a fight have the same power, neither Unit wins and both Units lose the fight.” Losers are defeated and go to the trash. One exception added in that update: “Units with power 0 cannot defeat a Unit in a fight,” so a power 0 Unit can lose a fight but never knock anything out.

How Many Gigs Does a Unit Steal?

It depends on power (9.20.1). A Unit with power 0 steals nothing. Power 1 to 9 steals 1 Gig, power 10 to 19 steals 2, and so on, one more for every 10 power. You choose which Gigs to take, whatever their value. In Welcome to Night City, four Units have power 10 or more and steal 2 Gigs from the start: Animals Wrecker (10), Placide: Voodoo Sentinel (10), Saul Bright: Stormrider (14) and Adam Smasher: Metal Over Meat (15). Eight Units have power 0, including Sketchy Ripper and Delamain: Rideshare AI, so they’re played for their effects, not for stealing.

Gear can push a Unit past these lines. Our Cyberpunk TCG Gear guide explains how equipped power adds up, and our how to play guide covers a full turn.

Unit Keywords: Blocker, Adrenaline and Triggers

A few keywords appear again and again on Units. The rules define each one as shorthand:

  • BLOCKER: “When a rival Unit attacks, you may spend this Unit to redirect the attack to it instead.” You use it during the React Step, and only with a ready Unit.
  • ADRENALINE: “This Unit can attack the turn it’s played.” Riding Nomad and Modded Kusanagi have it, and Valentino Street Racer can give it to another Unit with cost 5 or less.
  • PLAY: “When you play this card…” It’s the most common trigger, on 24 of the 69 Units.
  • ATTACK: “When this Unit attacks…”, which happens as you spend the Unit to declare the attack.
  • DEFEATED: “When this Unit, Legend, or Gear is defeated…”, such as Screw: Lovelorn Fool, which returns another Unit from your trash to your hand.

Red Cyberpunk TCG Units

Red’s Units are aggressive: a lot of power for the cost, Units that reward winning fights, and several that pay off Gigs with a value of 8 or more.

  • Johnny Silverhand: Never Stop Fighting (cost 6, power 8, Epic): the first time it wins a fight each turn, it readies, and it “wins all fights against CORPO Units”.
  • Royce: Don’t Call Me Simon (cost 5): when played, defeats a rival Unit with power 2 or less, or 3 or less if you have more Street Cred.
  • Yorinobu Arasaka: Steel Dragon (cost 7): when played, you may play a Unit with cost 4 or less from your hand or trash for free.
  • Animals Wrecker (cost 6, power 10): no text, but power 10 means it steals 2 Gigs.
  • Octant (cost 7): costs 1 €$ less for each friendly Gig with a value of 8 or more.

Yellow Cyberpunk TCG Units

Yellow has the most Units, and many of them are built around Gear: ripperdocs that find, replay or discount cyberware.

  • Adam Smasher: Metal Over Meat (cost 9, power 15, Epic): when played, defeats all other Units, including your own. It’s the most expensive and most powerful Unit in the set.
  • Viktor Vektor: Drop Your Illusions (cost 5, Epic): your first CYBERWARE Gear each turn costs 3 €$ less.
  • Viktor Vektor: You Might Feel a Little Pinch (cost 3): when played, plays a CYBERWARE Gear with cost 2 or less from your trash for free.
  • Jackie Welles: Ride or Die Choom (cost 6, Epic): +2 power when attacking for each friendly Gig with an even value, and draws for each odd one when defeated.
  • MaxTac Suppression Team (cost 5): rival Units can’t attack the turn they’re played, which shuts off Adrenaline and Go Solo attacks.

Green Cyberpunk TCG Units

Green’s Units work with Legends and readying. Several let you Call a Legend for free or ready your cards again.

  • Saul Bright: Stormrider (cost 8, power 14): other friendly Units have +2 power while attacking, and up to 3 friendly Units ready at the end of your turn.
  • Panam Palmer: Strength Through Family (cost 6, Secret Rare): during your turn you may Call a Legend for free, and when it attacks you may discard 1 to draw 1 for each friendly face-up Legend.
  • Sandayu Oda: Hanako’s Guardian (cost 7): when played, spends a rival Unit for each friendly value-pair of Gigs, and can attack rival Units the turn it’s played.
  • Maelstrom Zealots (cost 4, power 0): when it loses a fight, it defeats the opposing rival Unit.
  • Riding Nomad (cost 5): Adrenaline, so it can attack right away.

Blue Cyberpunk TCG Units

Blue’s Units draw cards, work with Programs and send rival Units to the bottom of the deck.

  • Judy Álvarez: Nothing to Doubt (cost 6, Secret Rare): pay 1 €$ and spend her to reveal the top card of your deck, then play it for free or add it to your hand.
  • Placide: Voodoo Sentinel (cost 8, power 10): when played and when it attacks, you may discard a Program to bottom-deck a rival Unit.
  • Lizzy Wizzy: Delicate Weapon (cost 5): when played, plays a Program with cost 3 or less from your hand or trash for free, and she has Blocker.
  • Delamain: Rideshare AI (cost 3, power 0): draw 2 when played.
  • Modded Kusanagi (cost 6, power 8): Adrenaline, but it returns to your hand at the end of your turn.

The Most Played Units in Official Decks

The five decklists WeirdCo published on November 6, 2026 run 78 Units out of 200 cards, 22 different ones. Mosh Pit! plays the most Units, 21, and Overwatch and Sasha Enjoyer the fewest, 12.

Two of the four most played Units have no rules text at all. Rockn’ Rockerboy is power 8 for cost 5 and Psycho Squad is power 6 for cost 4, which is a lot of power for the price. 48 of the 69 Retail Units aren’t in any of the five lists. Five decklists are a small sample from one week, so treat this as a starting point, not a ranking. See the full WeirdCo decklists for how each Unit fits its deck.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Units are in Cyberpunk TCG?

Welcome to Night City has 69 Units: 17 Red, 18 Yellow, 17 Green and 17 Blue. Four more, Minotaur, Goro Takemura, Dexter DeShawn and MT0D12 Flathead, are only in the starter decks.

Can a Unit attack the turn it’s played?

Not normally. Units enter the field with Lag, which stops them attacking until the end of the turn. A Unit with Adrenaline, or a card effect that says so, can attack right away.

What happens when two Units with the same power fight?

Neither wins and both lose, so both are defeated. The exception is a Unit with power 0, which can’t defeat a Unit in a fight.

How many Gigs does an attacking Unit steal?

Power 0 steals none, power 1 to 9 steals 1 Gig, and power 10 to 19 steals 2, adding one more for every extra 10 power.

What does Blocker do in Cyberpunk TCG?

When a rival Unit attacks, you may spend a ready Unit with Blocker to redirect the attack to it instead. Eight Units in Welcome to Night City have it.

What is the strongest Unit in Cyberpunk TCG?

By power, Adam Smasher: Metal Over Meat, with 15 for cost 9, followed by Saul Bright: Stormrider with 14. There’s no official ranking, and in WeirdCo’s decklists the most played Units are Placide, Rockn’ Rockerboy, Pepe Najarro and Psycho Squad.
