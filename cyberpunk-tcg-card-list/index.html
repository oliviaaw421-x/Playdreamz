Cyberpunk TCG Card List: Every Welcome to Night City Card by Color and Rarity

Welcome to Night City, the first Cyberpunk TCG set, has 140 cards plus 31 Iconic Rares. This Cyberpunk TCG card list shows every one of them with a picture you can tap to enlarge, sorted by color, with its card number, card type, rarity and cost, all checked against the official card database. It also covers all 20 Legends, the Iconic Rare checklist, the starter deck exclusives and how the Beta and Retail card numbers differ.

The short version: Welcome to Night City has 140 base cards, 35 in each of the four colors (Red, Yellow, Green and Blue), numbered 001 to 140. That includes 20 Legends, 69 Units, 17 Gear and 34 Programs. Cards 141 to 171 are the 31 Iconic Rares. The Retail set, launching November 6, 2026, uses the same card list and numbers as the Beta set.

Welcome to Night City at a Glance

Every card in the Welcome to Night City card list belongs to one of four colors, and each color has exactly 35 cards. Inside each color, the cards follow the same order: Legends first, then Units, Gear and Programs. The rarities are spread unevenly. Blue has two of the four Secret Rares, and Yellow has four Epic Rares.

By rarity, the 140 base cards are 60 Commons, 32 Uncommons, 32 Rares, 12 Epic Rares and 4 Secret Rares. Our Cyberpunk TCG rarity symbols guide shows what each symbol looks like and how often each rarity turns up in packs.

How Cyberpunk TCG Card Numbers Work

The card number is printed in the bottom-left corner of every card. The official rules say the card number “identifies a single card within a product,” so it is the quickest way to find a card in this list, in a binder or in an online listing.

  • 001 to 140: the base cards, grouped by color.
  • 141 to 171: the Iconic Rares, alternate-art versions of cards that already appear in 001 to 140.
  • 005a and 005b: V: Streetkid is the only base card printed with two artworks. Card 005a shows a male V and 005b a female V. They are the same card in a game, so a deck can hold three copies in any mix of the two.
  • β in front of the number: marks a Beta card, for example β109 instead of 109.

All 20 Cyberpunk TCG Legends

Legends are the heart of a Cyberpunk TCG deck. Every deck uses exactly three, and their RAM sets which colors your deck can use. Each color has five Legends, and every Legend in this set has 2 RAM in its own color. Some Legends also have a Go Solo cost, which lets you play them onto the field as a Unit. Our how to play guide explains how Legends work in a game.

Two Legends are Secret Rares: Johnny Silverhand: Rocking Renegade and Sasha Yakovleva: Won’t Let You Down. Three are Epic Rares, and the other 15 are Rares. The 18 Legends that aren’t Secret Rares each have a Legend Iconic Rare version too.

Red Cards (001–035)

Red opens the card list. It has 15 Commons, 9 Uncommons, 7 Rares, 3 Epic Rares and 1 Secret Rare. Its Legends include Adam Smasher: Ender of Legends, the Epic Rare Legend with the highest Go Solo cost in the set at 9, and the Secret Rare Johnny Silverhand: Rocking Renegade.

Yellow Cards (036–070)

Yellow has 16 Commons, 7 Uncommons, 8 Rares and 4 Epic Rares, the most Epic Rares of any color, but no Secret Rare. It also has the most Units (18) and Gear (6), and the fewest Programs (6).

Green Cards (071–105)

Green has 13 Commons, 11 Uncommons, 8 Rares, 2 Epic Rares and 1 Secret Rare, Panam Palmer: Strength Through Family. Several Arasaka cards are Green, including the Legends Goro Takemura: Vengeful Bodyguard and Hanako Arasaka: Daughter of the Emperor.

Blue Cards (106–140)

Blue closes the base card list. It has 16 Commons, 5 Uncommons, 9 Rares, 3 Epic Rares and 2 Secret Rares, more Secret Rares than any other color: Judy Álvarez: Nothing to Doubt and the Legend Sasha Yakovleva: Won’t Let You Down. It also has the most Programs (10).

Iconic Rare Checklist (141–171)

The Iconic Rares are foiled, alternate-art versions of cards from earlier in the set, so they are not new cards for deck building. They come in three groups: 18 Legend Iconic Rares (141–158), 9 Core Iconic Rares for Units, Gear and Programs (159–167) and 4 Secret Iconic Rares (168–171), the top chase cards in the set. Our guide to the most valuable Cyberpunk TCG cards covers what they sell for.

Starter Deck Exclusive Cards

The 140 base cards in this Cyberpunk TCG card list are everything you can pull from Welcome to Night City booster packs. The two starter decks add ten more cards that aren’t in boosters: each deck’s three Legends and two main-deck cards.

  • Embracing Power: Yorinobu Arasaka: Embracing Destruction, Goro Takemura: Hands Unclean, Saburo Arasaka: Stubborn Patriarch, Minotaur and Goro Takemura: Losing His Way.
  • The Heist: V: Corporate Exile, Viktor Vektor: Sit Down and Relax, Jackie Welles: Pour One Out For Me, Dexter DeShawn: One Last Chance and MT0D12 Flathead.

Nova Rare promos, such as box toppers and organized play prizes, are also listed separately from the main set in the official database. See our Cyberpunk TCG starter decks guide for both decklists.

Beta vs Retail Card Lists

The Beta set came first, through WeirdCo’s Kickstarter, and the Retail set arrives in stores on November 6, 2026. In the official database, the Cyberpunk TCG card list is the same for both versions: Welcome to Night City has the same 172 printings in Beta and Retail: the same card names, card numbers and rarities. The visible differences are:

  • Card numbers: Beta numbers start with β.
  • Iconic Rare art: some Retail Iconic Rares use different artwork from their Beta versions. Our Beta vs Retail comparison shows examples.

There is also a French Retail edition with the same card numbers. Prices can differ a lot between Beta and Retail copies of the same card, so always check which version a listing is selling.

How to Use This Cyberpunk TCG Card List

  • Checking a pull: find the card number in the bottom-left corner, then look it up in the color table of this card list. The rarity column tells you how rare it is.
  • Building a deck: start with three Legends from the Legends table. Their colors decide which color tables you can build from. A deck has a main deck of 40 to 50 cards and no more than 3 copies of any card with the same name and subtitle, as our deck building rules explain.
  • Collecting a full set: a complete base set is 140 cards (141 if you want both V: Streetkid artworks). A full master set adds the 31 Iconic Rares.
  • Selling or buying: search with the card name and card number, and say whether it is Beta or Retail.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many cards are in the Cyberpunk TCG first set?

Welcome to Night City has 140 base cards, numbered 001 to 140, plus 31 Iconic Rares numbered 141 to 171. V: Streetkid has two artworks, 005a and 005b, so the set has 172 printings in total.

How many Legends are in Cyberpunk TCG?

Welcome to Night City has 20 Legends, five in each color. The two starter decks add six more starter-only Legends, three per deck. Every deck uses exactly three Legends.

What are the four colors in Cyberpunk TCG?

Red, Yellow, Green and Blue. Each color has 35 cards in Welcome to Night City. A card’s color shows in the frame around its cost and in its RAM symbol, and your Legends’ RAM decides which colors your deck can use.

Is the Retail card list different from Beta?

No. The official database lists the same card names, numbers and rarities for both. Beta card numbers start with β, and some Iconic Rares have different art in Retail.

Why are there two V: Streetkid cards?

V: Streetkid is printed with two artworks: 005a shows a male V and 005b a female V. Both have the same rules text and count as the same card, so a deck can include up to three copies in any mix.

Where can I see every Cyberpunk TCG card?

The official Cyberpunk TCG website has a card database with images and rules text for every card. The Cyberpunk TCG card list in this guide covers all 140 base cards and 31 Iconic Rares from that database, sorted by color, with card numbers and rarities.
