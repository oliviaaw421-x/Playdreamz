Cyberpunk TCG Legends: All 26 Legends and What They Do

Cyberpunk TCG Legends are the famous Night City characters at the heart of every deck, from Johnny Silverhand and Judy Álvarez to Adam Smasher. Every deck runs exactly three of them, and they decide your colors, your RAM limits and a big part of how you play. This guide lists all 26 Cyberpunk TCG Legends released so far, with pictures and what each one does, and explains how Legends work, using the official card database and WeirdCo’s Comprehensive Rules.

The short version: there are 26 Legends: 20 in Welcome to Night City booster packs (five per color) and six that only come in the two starter decks. Your three Legends start face-down. Once per turn you can pay 1 €$ to Call one face-up, and eight Legends with a cost can Go Solo onto the field as a Unit. Everything here is as of November 8, 2026.

All 26 Cyberpunk TCG Legends at a Glance

Welcome to Night City, the first set, has 20 Cyberpunk TCG Legends, five in each of the four colors. The two starter decks add three more Legends each. Those six aren’t in booster packs, which is why Green and Blue end up with seven Legends while Red and Yellow have six.

In booster packs, 15 of the 20 Legends are Rares, three are Epics (Adam Smasher, Panam Palmer and Judy Álvarez) and two are Secret Rares (Johnny Silverhand and Sasha Yakovleva). All six starter deck Legends are Epics. Every booster Legend also has an Iconic Rare version with special art, numbered 141 to 158, plus 168 and 169 for the two Secret Rares. Our rarity guide covers how often those turn up.

What Are Legends in Cyberpunk TCG?

Legends are one of the four Cyberpunk TCG card types, next to Units, Gear and Programs. They have a different cardback from the rest of your cards, and they never go in your main deck. A legal deck is exactly 3 Legends plus a main deck of 40 to 50 other cards. Here is what the Comprehensive Rules say about them:

  • They start face-down. At setup you shuffle your three Legends and place them face-down in your Legends area in a random order, so you don’t know which one you’ll flip first. Your rival may cut or shuffle them.
  • They pay for things. You can spend any Legend in your Legends area to pay 1 €$, face-up or face-down. A face-up Legend needs a Sell Tag to do this, and every Legend released so far has one. Legends still aren’t Eddies.
  • They set your RAM limits. Every Legend so far has 2 RAM in its own color. Add up your three Legends’ RAM in each color, and every other card in your deck must fit within those limits.
  • No two with the same name. You can’t use two Legends that share a name. V: Streetkid and V: Corporate Exile are both named “V”, so they can’t go in the same deck, and the same goes for the two Goro Takemuras and the two Jackie Welles.
  • They can’t go anywhere else. A Legend is only allowed in the Legends area or on the field. If one ever moves to your hand, deck or trash, it’s removed from the game.

The player who goes first spends their two left-most Legends at the start, and they don’t ready on that first turn. Our how to play guide walks through the full setup and turn.

How to Call a Legend in Cyberpunk TCG

To turn a Legend face-up, you Call it. During your main phase you pay 1 €$ and flip one of your face-down Legends. The official Gameplay Guide tells players not to peek first, because the random order is part of the game. A few rules matter:

  • Once per turn. You can only Call a Legend once each turn, even if a card tells you to Call one.
  • On your rival’s turn too. You may also Call a Legend as a reaction when a rival Unit attacks, if you still have an unspent Eddie or Legend to pay with.
  • It keeps its orientation. A spent Legend stays spent when you flip it, and a ready one stays ready.
  • {Call} effects trigger. Text after the Call icon happens when you flip that Legend through Call a Legend. V: Streetkid, Dexter DeShawn, Dum Dum, Muamar Reyes, Padre, Wakako Okada and Viktor Vektor all have one.
  • Face-up Legends work for you. While a Legend is face-up in your Legends area, you can use its activated effects during your main phase. Effects with the {Spend} icon mean you turn that Legend sideways to use them. Effects marked {Quick} can also be used as a reaction to a rival’s attack.

What Does Go Solo Do in Cyberpunk TCG?

Most Cyberpunk TCG Legends show a dash where the cost would be. That is a null cost, and a Legend with a null cost can never be played to the field. Eight Legends have a real cost, and all eight have the Go Solo keyword: “Pay this Legend’s cost to play it as a ready Unit. It can attack this turn. If it leaves the field, remove it from the game.”

So a Go Solo Legend is a big Unit you can drop in once it’s face-up and attack with straight away, without waiting out Lag. The catch is that it’s gone for good if it’s defeated or leaves the field, and you lose it as a 1 €$ payment in your Legends area. On the field it counts as both a Unit and a Legend.

Sasha Yakovleva is the odd one: her printed power is 0, but when she attacks you reveal the top card of your deck, add it to your hand and she gains power equal to its cost. V: Corporate Exile has no other text at all, just Go Solo with 8 power for 5 €$.

Red Cyberpunk TCG Legends

Red has three of the eight Go Solo Legends, including Adam Smasher, the one with the highest cost and power.

  • Adam Smasher: Ender of Legends (Epic): Go Solo for 9, with 9 power. When he’s played, defeat a rival Unit.
  • Dexter DeShawn: Off the Grid: {Call} choose one: give a friendly Unit +2 power this turn, or draw 1. {Spend}: increase a Gig by up to 2.
  • Johnny Silverhand: Rocking Renegade (Secret Rare): 2 €$ and {Spend}: a friendly Unit can attack spent rival Units the turn it’s played. If it’s a ROCKER Unit, it also gets +2 power this turn. The effect costs 1 €$ less for each friendly Gig with a value of 8 or more.
  • Royce: Psycho on the Edge: Go Solo for 6, with 6 power. During your turn he has +2 power for each Gear equipped to him.
  • V: Streetkid: {Call} trash 3 (move the top 3 cards of your deck to the trash), then add a BRAINDANCE Program from your trash to your hand. Go Solo for 5, with 6 power. There are two artworks: 005a shows male V and 005b female V.

Yellow Cyberpunk TCG Legends

All five Yellow booster Legends are Rares. Several of them reward Gear (see our Gear guide) and Gig dice rolls.

  • Dum Dum: Maelstrom Triggerman: {Call} you may defeat a friendly Gear; if you do, draw 2, otherwise draw 1. {Quick} 1 €$ and {Spend}: give a friendly Unit +1 power this turn for each Gear equipped to it.
  • Kerry Eurodyne: Axe, Attitude, Audience: when you roll in a Gig from your fixer area, you may ignore the result and reroll it once. When you roll a Gig’s minimum or maximum value, draw 1, or draw 3 if it’s a d20.
  • Muamar Reyes: El Capitán: {Call} choose one: a friendly Unit can’t be defeated in a fight this turn, or draw 1. {Spend}: adjust a Gig by 1.
  • River Ward: Detective on the Hunt: {Quick} {Spend}: play a Gear with cost 2 or less from your hand for free. When a friendly equipped Unit is defeated, look at the top 2 cards of your deck and trash 1.
  • Rogue Amendiares: Preem Solo: Go Solo for 7, with 7 power. When a friendly Legend steals a Gig, draw 1 if its value is even, or make a rival discard 1 if it’s odd.

Green Cyberpunk TCG Legends

Green leans on value-pairs (two Gig dice showing the same value) and on protecting your Units.

  • Goro Takemura: Vengeful Bodyguard: {Quick} 1 €$ and {Spend}: give a friendly Unit with cost 4 or less {Blocker} this turn, and +1 power too if you control a value-pair of Gigs. When a friendly Unit uses Blocker, you may discard 1 to draw 1.
  • Hanako Arasaka: Daughter of the Emperor: {Spend}: swap a friendly Gig with a rival Gig. At the start of your turn, draw 1 for each friendly value-pair of Gigs.
  • Jackie Welles: Mama’s Favorite: Go Solo for 6, with 8 power. If a friendly Unit would be defeated, you may pay 1 €$ to defeat Jackie instead.
  • Padre: Man of the Cross: {Call} choose one: spend a rival Unit, or draw 1. {Spend}: set a player’s Gig to the same value as another player’s Gig.
  • Panam Palmer: Nomad Cavalry (Epic): 2 €$ and {Spend}: move a Gear from Panam to an unequipped friendly Unit and ready that Unit. At the end of your turn, if 5 or more friendly Units and/or Legends are equipped, ready them.

Blue Cyberpunk TCG Legends

Two of the Blue Legends, Alt Cunningham and Judy Álvarez, are built around Programs, and Blue also has Sasha Yakovleva.

  • Alt Cunningham: Soulkiller Architect: {Spend}: your next Program this turn costs 1 €$ less for each friendly min Gig (a die at its lowest value), to a minimum of 1 €$. 1 €$ and {Spend}: play a Program from your trash, paying its cost, then bottom-deck it.
  • Evelyn Parker: Beautiful Enigma: when a friendly CORPO or GANGER Unit steals one or more Gigs, ready 1 Eddie. 1 €$ and {Spend}: a rival Unit must attack next turn if it can.
  • Judy Álvarez: Braindance Maestro (Epic): when you play a BRAINDANCE Program, give a friendly Unit +1 power this turn. {Spend}: trash the top card of your deck; if it’s a Program, you may add it to your hand.
  • Sasha Yakovleva: Won’t Let You Down (Secret Rare): Go Solo for 5. When she attacks, reveal the top card of your deck, add it to your hand and give her power equal to its cost this turn. When she’s defeated, a rival discards 1.
  • Wakako Okada: Peace and Harmony: {Call} choose one: give a rival Unit −2 power this turn, or draw 1. {Spend}: decrease a Gig by up to 2.

Cyberpunk TCG Starter Deck Legends

Six Legends come only in the two starter decks, three in each. You can’t pull their Epic versions from booster packs, but their Nova Rare versions are the six booster box toppers. Embracing Power is an Arasaka deck, and The Heist follows V and Jackie. Our starter decks guide has both full decklists.

  • Yorinobu Arasaka: Embracing Destruction (Red): the first time a friendly ARASAKA Unit attacks each turn, draw 1. Then, if you have less than 20 Street Cred, discard 1.
  • Goro Takemura: Hands Unclean (Green): Go Solo for 5, with 7 power and {Blocker}, so he can redirect a rival’s attack to himself.
  • Saburo Arasaka: Stubborn Patriarch (Green): friendly ARASAKA Units have +1 power while attacking.
  • Viktor Vektor: Sit Down and Relax (Yellow): {Call} look at the top 5 cards of your deck, reveal up to 2 Gears with cost 2 or less and add them to your hand, then bottom-deck the rest in a random order.
  • Jackie Welles: Pour One Out For Me (Blue): the first time you play a Blue Unit or Blue Gear each turn, you may decrease a friendly Gig by up to 2. If it becomes a min Gig, draw 1.
  • V: Corporate Exile (Blue): Go Solo for 5, with 8 power and no other text.

The Four Fixer Legends

Each Cyberpunk TCG color has one Legend with the Fixer tag, and the four are built on the same template. When you Call one, you choose between a small effect and drawing a card. Once it’s face-up, you can spend it to change a Gig die’s value. Being able to nudge Gigs matters because Units steal Gigs, and Gig values add up to the Street Cred you need to win.

Because they share a structure but have different names, you can run three of them together. One of WeirdCo’s own decklists, Triple Fixers, does exactly that with Dexter, Muamar and Wakako.

Which Cyberpunk TCG Legends Should You Pick?

Your Legends come first when you build a Cyberpunk TCG deck, because their colors set your RAM limits. Three Legends of one color give you 6 RAM in that color and nothing else. Mixing colors spreads the RAM out: a color covered by only one Legend has a limit of 2, so you can only play that color’s cards with 2 RAM or less. Sasha Yakovleva, V: Streetkid and Muamar Reyes each appear in two of the five decks. A few things to weigh when you pick your own:

  • Go Solo or not. A Go Solo Legend is a big threat, but it’s gone once it leaves the field. Legends with {Spend} effects keep working turn after turn from the Legends area.
  • Your first flip is random. You won’t know which Legend you Call first, so three Legends that all fit your plan are safer than one key Legend and two fillers.
  • Tags and keywords. Some Legends only help certain cards, such as BRAINDANCE Programs for V: Streetkid and Judy, ROCKER Units for Johnny, or ARASAKA Units for Saburo and Yorinobu. Build the main deck around them.

Every Version of a Cyberpunk TCG Legend

Most Cyberpunk TCG Legends have several printings with the same rules text, so any of them is legal in a deck. For a booster pack Legend, the official database lists:

  • The regular card, a Rare, Epic or Secret Rare in Welcome to Night City packs.
  • An Iconic Rare with special art, also from packs: Iconic Legend for 18 of them, Iconic Secret for Johnny and Sasha.
  • Pre-release versions for some Legends, from the Retail Pre-release and Beta Event kits.
  • Edgerunner Open promos. Dexter, V: Streetkid, Muamar and Judy have four extra versions each: alt art, then Regional, Finalist and Champion stamps.
  • An Encrypted Legend, a serial-numbered Nova Rare. All 26 Legends have one, given to the best-placed player using that Legend at an Edgerunner Open.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Legends are in Cyberpunk TCG?

There are 26 Legends so far: 20 in Welcome to Night City booster packs, five in each color, and six that only come in the Embracing Power and The Heist starter decks.

How many Legends do you need in a deck?

Exactly three. A legal Cyberpunk TCG deck has 3 Legends plus a main deck of 40 to 50 other cards, and no two of your Legends can share a name.

How do you flip a Legend face-up?

You Call it: pay 1 €$ during your main phase to flip one of your face-down Legends. You can do this once per turn, and also as a reaction when a rival Unit attacks.

Can you use V: Streetkid and V: Corporate Exile together?

No. Both are named V, and the rules don’t allow two Legends with the same name in one deck. The same applies to the two Goro Takemura and the two Jackie Welles Legends.

Which Legends can be played as Units?

Only the eight Legends with Go Solo: V: Streetkid, Sasha Yakovleva, V: Corporate Exile, Goro Takemura: Hands Unclean, Royce, Jackie Welles: Mama’s Favorite, Rogue Amendiares and Adam Smasher. The others have a null cost and can’t be played to the field.

Are Legends in booster packs?

Yes, the 20 Welcome to Night City Legends are, as Rares, Epics, Secret Rares and Iconic Rares. The six starter deck Legends aren’t in packs, but their Nova Rare versions come as booster box toppers.
